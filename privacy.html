Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-28

Derloyal Postiz is a personal, self-hosted social media scheduling tool operated by Volkan Ocakdan (derloyal). This policy explains how it handles data.

Data collected

The app stores OAuth access tokens for social media accounts (such as TikTok) that the operator connects, solely to schedule and publish the operator's own content to those accounts.

Data storage

All data is stored on the operator's own self-hosted server. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Data use

Connected account tokens are used exclusively to publish content on behalf of the operator, as authorized through each platform's official OAuth flow.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Volcakdan@yahoo.de.
